## Readiness criteria

Before diving into service page strategy, ensure your team is ready. Start by confirming the owner of the strategy. This person will be responsible for driving the process and ensuring everyone is aligned.

Next, identify the required inputs. These could be data, resources, or stakeholder approvals. Make sure these are available before proceeding.

Clearly define the expected outcome. This will serve as a benchmark to measure success later on.

Establish decision criteria. These are the metrics or factors that will guide your strategy. They should be specific, measurable, and relevant to your goals.

Lastly, identify the first metric that will show the strategy is working. This will help you track progress and make data-driven decisions.

## Validation checks

After implementation, validate your service page strategy with these checks:

1. **User Engagement**: Measure user engagement metrics such as bounce rate, time on page, and click-through rate. High engagement indicates a successful strategy.

2. **Conversion Rate**: Track conversion rates to see if your strategy is driving the desired actions, such as form submissions or purchases.

3. **Customer Feedback**: Gather customer feedback through surveys or reviews. This will provide valuable insights into what's working and what's not.

4. **SEO Performance**: Monitor your search engine rankings and organic traffic. An effective strategy should improve your visibility online.
